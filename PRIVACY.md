## What this notice covers

This notice describes the public Postall project website hosted through GitHub
Pages and the browser-based features on that site. It does not yet describe a
hosted Postall subscription service. As of the date above, the site has no
checkout and does not collect payment-card or bank details. Publish a revised
notice before launching a hosted service or introducing accounts, analytics,
forms, or other data collection.

## Information processed by the website

- **Product-guide messages:** The guide runs in the visitor's browser. It uses
  the message to choose a locally defined answer. Messages are not sent to a
  Postall chat server or saved by the guide.
- **Preferences:** The site stores the selected theme and whether the
  deployment-information notice was dismissed in browser `localStorage`.
- **Email:** Selecting an email link opens the visitor's own email application.
  The visitor chooses whether to send information to the project.
- **Hosting and delivery:** GitHub Pages delivers this site. GitHub may process
  visitor and technical information under its own terms and privacy notices.
- **Third-party web assets:** The page loads Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome from
  third-party content-delivery networks. Those providers may receive ordinary
  connection data, such as an IP address, when a browser requests an asset.
  Review their current privacy notices and consider hosting these assets
  locally if that data flow is not wanted.

The site should not be described as analytics-free or as making no third-party
requests without rechecking its code and deployed version. This notice reflects
the site features identified when this draft was prepared.

## Self-hosted software and connected services

The setup scripts install a Docker image on a host chosen by the user and mount
a Docker volume for application data. A self-hosted instance may process
information entered into or connected to that instance. The person or
organization operating that instance is responsible for its configuration,
access controls, retention, backups, and legal obligations. Social networks,
AI providers, hosting providers, and other services connected by the operator
have their own data practices and terms.

The public website and this repository do not provide enough information to
state exactly what data every version of the application collects, where
application telemetry goes, or how every integration handles information.
Verify those details against the actual application and infrastructure before
making privacy promises or using it with personal or client data.

## Purposes and legal basis

For the current public site, the purposes are to display project information,
provide a browser-based product guide, remember display preferences, and allow
visitors to contact the project. Determine and document the lawful basis,
notice, consent, and any other requirements that apply to the actual processing
under Indian law and any other law that applies to your visitors.

## Retention and security

The theme and notice preferences remain in the visitor's browser until cleared
by that visitor. Email correspondence may remain in the sender's and recipient's
mail systems. GitHub and the CDN providers may retain technical information
under their own policies. The project owner should set and publish a retention
period for project-controlled correspondence and implement appropriate
administrative and technical safeguards.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment credentials, or other secrets in
support messages.

## Rights, requests, and complaints

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correct,
erase, withdraw consent, or complain about the processing of their personal
data. Contact contact.postall@gmail.com to submit a request or grievance. The
project owner should publish a process, response timelines, identity checks,
and any escalation contact required by applicable law.

## Children

The project owner should assess the applicable age and parental-consent rules
before directing the website or any hosted service to children or processing
children's personal data.

## Changes and applicable law

This notice may be updated when the site, software, or applicable requirements
change. The project owner should date each revision and identify material
changes. Indian privacy requirements, including the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023 and rules or commencement notifications made under it,
should be reviewed with an Indian privacy lawyer before launch. The scope and
commencement of legal duties may depend on current notifications and the facts
of the service.
