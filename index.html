Privacy-First AI

Powered by Local LLMs. Zero Cloud API Fees.

Stop leaking strategic content and audience prompts to third-party providers. Postall interfaces directly with local runtimes like Ollama so you can generate copy, schedule multi-platform posts, and optimize campaigns entirely on your private infrastructure.

  • Complete Air-Gapped Privacy: Prompts never leave your machine or private server.
  • Zero Rate Limits: Iterate content models with unlimited inferences.
  • Custom Models: Use Llama 3, Mistral, Gemma, or local fine-tunes.

Absolute Data Sovereignty

Your Data Stays Yours. Period.

Unlike third-party SaaS schedulers that store your social credentials and brand strategies on central cloud clusters, Postall runs strictly under your direct custody using encrypted local volume mounts.

  • Zero Vendor Lock-in: Access or migrate your SQLite database anytime.
  • Isolated State Guard: Docker named volumes isolate records from crashes.
  • Local Secret Vault: OAuth tokens and keys never leave your container.
